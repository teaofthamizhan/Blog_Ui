Our Story

Founded with a passion for preserving Tamil tea heritage, My Tea of Thamizhan brings authentic, high-quality teas directly from the hills of Tamil Nadu to your cup. We believe every tea leaf carries the story of the land, the people, and generations of tradition.

Our commitment to sustainable farming and ethical sourcing ensures that every purchase supports local communities and protects our precious ecosystems.
