About My Tea of Thamizhan

Preserving heritage, one cup at a time

Our Story

My Tea of Thamizhan is a proud custodian of Tamil tea heritage. Founded with a passion for authentic flavors and traditional tea culture, we bring the rich legacy of Tamil Nadu's tea estates directly to your home.

Our journey began in the misty hills of the Nilgiris, where we discovered the magic of handpicked tea leaves. Every blend we create tells a story—of the soil, the climate, the people, and generations of expertise.

Our Mission

🌱 Sustainability

We partner with farmers who practice sustainable and ethical tea cultivation, protecting our ecosystems for future generations.

💚 Community

Fair trade practices ensure our farming partners receive proper compensation and support for their livelihoods.

✨ Quality

We hand-select only the finest leaves and craft unique blends that celebrate Tamil tea traditions.

📚 Heritage

Through storytelling and education, we keep Tamil tea culture alive and vibrant in the modern world.
